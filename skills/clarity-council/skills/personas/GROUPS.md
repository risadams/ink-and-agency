# Persona Groups

Each group below is a small, purpose-built panel for focused council sessions. Reference a group by
its **slug** (the `` `code` `` after the title). To use one, expand it into its listed personas and
consult each, then synthesize.

## Product Delivery Core &nbsp;`product-delivery-core`

Use for prioritization, scope, and delivery tradeoffs.

- product-owner
- product-manager
- scrum-master
- tech-lead
- qa-engineer

## Platform and Reliability &nbsp;`platform-and-reliability`

Use for architecture, operations, risk, and production stability.

- senior-architect
- ops-architect
- devops-engineer
- site-reliability-engineer
- security-expert

## Growth and Content &nbsp;`growth-and-content`

Use for audience, messaging, distribution, and conversion work.

- growth-strategist
- social-media-marketer
- content-creator
- customer-advocate
- technical-writer

## Research and Knowledge &nbsp;`research-and-knowledge`

Use for evidence gathering, analysis, and durable knowledge capture.

- researcher
- statistics-expert
- knowledge-manager
- data-engineer
- technical-writer

## Governance and Resourcing &nbsp;`governance-and-resourcing`

Use for funding, policy, risk, and organizational constraints.

- business-owner
- financial-officer
- risk-manager
- compliance-officer
- finops-engineer

## Personal Productivity &nbsp;`personal-productivity`

Use for daily briefings, focus-block proposals, calendar/task triage, and any "what do I do next" framing.

- personal-assistant
- psychologist
- scrum-master

## Communication and Tone &nbsp;`communication-and-tone`

Use for decoding messages, drafting/softening responses, and reviewing tone of comments before they post.

- psychologist
- customer-advocate
- technical-writer
- content-creator

## Investigation and Diagnosis &nbsp;`investigation-and-diagnosis`

Use for bug triage, root-cause hypothesis, and feature analysis where evidence-gathering rigor matters more than speed.

- researcher
- senior-architect
- qa-engineer
- devils-advocate

## Stakeholder Reporting &nbsp;`stakeholder-reporting`

Use for sprint reviews, status updates, and any artifact destined for leadership or external audiences. Pairs visual rigor with durable-knowledge capture.

- statistics-expert
- infographics-expert
- product-owner
- knowledge-manager
- scrum-master

## Accessibility and Inclusivity &nbsp;`accessibility-and-inclusivity`

Use for slides, dashboards, public docs, and anywhere readability/contrast/alt-text matter.

- accessibility-expert
- ux-designer
- graphic-designer
- technical-writer

## Convenience panels

Broad, general-purpose archetypes for when a domain-specific group above doesn't fit.

### Technical Focus &nbsp;`technical-focus`

All the engineering voices — architecture, implementation, quality, ops, security, plus a skeptic.

- senior-architect
- senior-developer
- tech-lead
- qa-engineer
- devops-engineer
- security-expert
- devils-advocate

### Stakeholder Focus &nbsp;`stakeholder-focus`

The business/delivery voices — value, customers, budget, compliance, culture, plus a skeptic.

- product-owner
- customer-advocate
- business-owner
- compliance-officer
- culture-lead
- devils-advocate

### Full Council &nbsp;`full-council`

Everyone in **technical-focus** plus **stakeholder-focus** — the widest panel. Use sparingly; large
councils dilute synthesis. Prefer a focused group unless the decision genuinely spans both domains.

- senior-architect
- senior-developer
- tech-lead
- qa-engineer
- devops-engineer
- security-expert
- product-owner
- customer-advocate
- business-owner
- compliance-officer
- culture-lead
- devils-advocate

### Quick Pulse &nbsp;`quick-pulse`

A fast three-voice gut-check when you want a spread of perspectives without a full session.

- product-owner
- senior-architect
- devils-advocate

### Estimation &nbsp;`estimation`

Scrum-poker style sizing — complexity, delivery, and quality risk.

- scrum-master
- tech-lead
- senior-developer
- qa-engineer
